# The garage

The garage is where you choose what goes on the roof. Open it from the main menu, change weapon with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, and **Select** to take it to the arena. The choice carries over to your next match.

## Razor

A 4×4 war rig, balanced: *a well-rounded war machine built for the scrapyard*. It is the one machine for now.

- **Hull**: 100 points
- **Weight**: 1,400 kg
- **Weapon mount**: a turret on the roof that turns to your aim

## Weapons

| | Minigun | Rocket Pod |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | 4 a round | 38 on a direct hit, less towards the blast's edge |
| Rate of fire | 12 rounds a second | 2 rockets a second |
| Magazine | 60 rounds | 6 rockets |
| Reload | 2.2 s | 3.5 s |
| Range | 160 m | 220 m |
| Projectile | instant hit | rocket at 95 m/s, 5 m blast |
| Sustained damage | about 33 a second | about 35 a second, if every rocket lands |

Sustained damage counts whole magazines with their reloads.

### Minigun

Six spinning barrels. It hits the moment you fire, spreads a little, and keeps the pressure on: one belt takes five seconds to empty. Best close in and against machines that dodge.

### Rocket Pod

Six unguided rockets. Slow to reload and they need leading, but a blast hurts everything within 5 m and shoves cars sideways, so a near miss still does damage. Three direct hits wreck a Razor.
