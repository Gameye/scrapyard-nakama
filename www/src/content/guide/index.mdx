# Guide

Scrapyard is car combat in your browser. You drive an armoured war rig, fire the gun on its roof, and try to wreck more machines than wreck you before the ten-minute clock runs out. Play online in **Classic**, where bots fill the seats nobody has taken, or practise against bots on your own.

The game's menus keep their words short. This guide has the rest:

- [How to play](/docs/how-to-play): from the main menu to your first wreck, and what happens after yours.
- [Controls](/docs/controls): every key, in a match and in the menus.
- [Modes](/docs/modes): Team Deathmatch and Free for All, with clocks, overtime, respawns, scoring and pickups.
- [Arenas](/docs/arenas): the Scrapyard and The City, where the teams start and where the hot zones are.
- [The garage](/docs/garage): the Razor, the Minigun and the Rocket Pod, with numbers.
- [FAQ](/docs/faq): browsers, guests and accounts, and what the site keeps about you.
