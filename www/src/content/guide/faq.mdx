# FAQ

## Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy.

## What do I need to play?

A desktop browser with WebGL 2 and WebAssembly (current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari have both), a keyboard and a mouse. Phones and tablets aren't supported: the game needs keys and a mouse, and on a device without a mouse or trackpad it shows a notice instead of starting.

If the game says it couldn't start graphics, turn on hardware acceleration in your browser's settings, or try another browser.

## Do I need an account?

No. **Play** signs you in as a guest and opens the game. An account keeps one name on every browser you play from; [create one](/register) whenever you like.

## Can I play online against other people?

Yes. On the arena screen, pick a mode, then **Classic**: Find Match. The game server finds you other players of that mode and picks the arena: a new match once enough people are searching (eight at once, down to any two after 30 seconds), or a free seat in a match already on. Accept the match found within 10 seconds (<kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Y</kbd>). A match holds eight machines: people take over bots' seats, and bots on Normal fill the rest. In Team Deathmatch you join the side with fewer people.

You can practise against bots while you search; the match found shows over the practice match. The search keeps going if you reload the page, and moves with you if you search from another tab.

You play under your account's username, or as a guest, named Guest and four characters. The gun you picked in the garage comes with you. When the results are up, the next match starts by itself 15 seconds later.

**Practice** is still there: you against bots, at the difficulty you pick.

## What does the site keep about me?

For an account: your email address, your username and display name, and your password, which the game server stores only as a hash. A guest account is tied to an ID kept in your browser. Your game settings stay in your browser too.

When you play online, the match server's log notes your account ID, your name in the match and your IP address as you join and leave. Nothing from the match itself is stored after it ends.

You can change your username, display name and password, or delete your account, on your [account page](/account). Deleting it removes it from the game server.

## The game runs slowly. What can I do?

Open **Settings → Graphics** in the game. Lower the resolution scale first, then the quality preset. Closing other heavy tabs helps too.
